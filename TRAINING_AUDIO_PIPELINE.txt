TRAINING_AUDIO_PIPELINE.txt
============================
Training mode was changed to a strict one-utterance-at-a-time pipeline:

1. Child speaks.
2. VAD detects end of speech.
3. Microphone is immediately hard-muted.
4. Whisper decodes only that captured utterance.
5. Exactly one feedback response is played: "أحسنت" or "حاول مرة أخرى".
6. Microphone remains muted until the response finishes.
7. The next target starts only after the response.

This prevents the previous microphone/TTS overlap and stale second-utterance problem.
The default VAD end silence was reduced to 220 ms, minimum voiced time to 180 ms,
minimum vowel time to 90 ms, and speech start to 100 ms for faster response.
